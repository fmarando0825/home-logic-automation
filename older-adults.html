A DIFFERENT APPROACH

Stay connected without hovering.

The goal isn’t constant surveillance. It’s better awareness when something meaningful happens.

Late-night door event A loved one can receive a smart alert if an exterior door opens during a time that is unusual for that household.
Nighttime movement Pathway lighting can come on automatically without requiring a phone, app, or wall switch.
One simple screen Frequently used home controls can be reduced to a few large, easy-to-understand choices.
Remote reassurance Family can check selected home status remotely without needing to call just to ask whether everything is okay.

PRIVACY FIRST

Technology should support independence, not take it away.

We design around the household’s comfort level. Camera-based features should be used only with clear consent and in appropriate areas of the home. Whenever possible, we favor event-based alerts and simple sensors over unnecessary continuous monitoring.
